Daily — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · App: Daily (com.aaronalex.daily) · Developer: Aaron Alex Sequeira

Short version: Daily keeps what it records on your phone. It only sends data off the phone for the features you choose: an optional sign-in (Google, Apple or email), Daily Plus, which writes a story of your day, and on iPhone, Sunny's reminders in the Dynamic Island. We never see your messages, notifications, photos or what's on your screen. We don't sell data, show ads or use analytics or tracking SDKs.

What Daily records on your phone

With your permission (Android "Usage access"), Daily reads which apps you used, for how long, how often you picked up your phone and when your screen was on. It also stores your goals, reminders, moods you log, water breaks and the reports it writes. All of this is stored only on your device, kept for the period you choose in Settings (90 days by default) and removed with Settings → Delete everything or by uninstalling the app. App backups to the cloud are turned off.

On iPhone, Daily uses Apple's Screen Time with your permission. Apple never gives Daily a list of the apps you used: your Screen Time is drawn inside Daily by Apple's own Screen Time extension and stays on your phone. Daily only learns your total phone time in steps (for example "1 hour reached at 10:05"), which it stores on your phone like the rest of your day.

Daily does not read notification content, messages, contacts, location, photos, microphone or screen content.

Service providers

  • Supabase — account and backend hosting
  • OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to — writing Daily Plus stories
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Google — Google Play billing and Google sign-in
  • Apple — App Store billing, Sign in with Apple, Screen Time and push notifications

We share data only with these providers, only for the purposes above. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Your choices

  • Use Daily without signing in and without Daily Plus: then nothing about your day leaves your phone.
  • Turn off Usage access in Android settings, or Screen Time access for Daily in iPhone Settings, at any time; Daily stops recording.
  • Export everything Daily stored (Settings → Export my data) or delete it (Settings → Delete everything).
  • Sign out in Settings → Account.
  • To delete your account and all server-side data (account ID, sign-in details, Plus cache, usage counts and Dynamic Island schedule), email aaronalexsequeira@gmail.com from the email you signed in with, or include the account ID. We delete it within 30 days. Cancel any subscription in Google Play or the App Store first; Google, Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records as required by law.

Children

Daily is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app for significant changes.
