## Description
Daily shows you what you did with your day, and helps you put the phone down a little more often.

SEE YOUR DAY
• Your Screen Time on one calm screen: total phone time, pickups, where your time went and your busiest hours.
• A weekly view with your daily average, how it compares with last week, and your top apps.

MEET SUNNY
• Sunny glows when your phone time stays low and slowly dims as you pass your goal.
• Sunny lives in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen: your progress, the next reminder, and a countdown on your breaks.

GENTLE REMINDERS
• Water after every hour on your phone, lunch, tea and dinner, or your own reminders.
• Take a break, Done or Snooze, right from the Dynamic Island.
• Short, animated breaks: stretch, sip, breathe.

YOUR DAY, WRITTEN
• Every evening Daily writes a short report of your day: the highlights, a sentence about your week and a small plan for tomorrow.
• Export it as a PDF.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your Screen Time stays on your iPhone: Apple's Screen Time draws it inside Daily, and Daily never gets a list of your apps.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

DAILY PLUS (optional subscription)
• A warm, AI-written story of your day and your week, with rewrites.
• Monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial on yearly. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews
  automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel it in Settings → your
  name → Subscriptions.

## Notes for App Review
- **Screen Time:** Daily uses the Family Controls (individual) authorization, approved for this team. To see the Screen
  Time view:
  1. Tap "Turn on Screen Time" on Today and allow access with Face ID.
  2. In "What counts?", tick All Apps & Categories and tap Done.

  Apple draws the report inside Daily (DeviceActivityReport); Daily receives no app list.
- **Dynamic Island:** it starts when Daily is opened. To see a reminder:
  1. Go to Reminders and add one for 2 minutes from now.
  2. Wait for it, then open Daily.
  3. The Island shows Take a break, Done and Snooze.
