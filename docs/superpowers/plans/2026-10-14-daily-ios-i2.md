# Daily for iPhone — Phase i2 (Screen Time) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** With the user's permission, Daily shows their real Screen Time on Today and Week. It also learns phone time in steps (milestones), so Sunny's mood, the Today headline and the Dynamic Island follow real phone time.

**Architecture:**
- **Swift module `DailyScreenTime`** (in the DailyNative pod) handles:
  - Family Controls authorization (individual)
  - the "What counts?" picker sheet
  - the saved selection (App Group)
  - DeviceActivity monitoring with one threshold event per milestone
  - reading the milestones
  - a native view hosting `DeviceActivityReport`
- **Two extensions**, built by `@bacons/apple-targets`:
  - `ActivityMonitor` (`device-activity-monitor`) records each milestone in the App Group, with a guard: phone time can never grow faster than the clock.
  - `ScreenTimeReport` (`device-activity-report`, added to apple-targets by a pnpm patch) draws Today and Week in SwiftUI in Daily's palette. Its data never leaves the extension.
- **JS:** pure milestone maths (thresholds, today's phone minutes, mood). Today, the Island, onboarding and Settings are wired to them.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-daily-ios-design.md` §1. Family Controls (Distribution) is approved for the account, and the user enabled it on `com.aaronalex.daily`, `.ActivityMonitor` and `.ScreenTimeReport`.

## Global Constraints

- **Identifiers:**
  - bundle IDs `com.aaronalex.daily.ActivityMonitor` and `com.aaronalex.daily.ScreenTimeReport` (exactly as registered)
  - App Group `group.com.aaronalex.daily`
- **Entitlements:** `com.apple.developer.family-controls: true` on the app and on both extensions.
- **Milestones:** every 10% of the goal up to 150%, plus every full hour up to 12 h. Event names are `m_<minutes>`.
- **Privacy:** the report's data stays in the extension. Milestones hold only minutes and timestamps.
- **Android:** nothing changes, and `pnpm test` stays green.

## Review Focus

1. **iOS fires DeviceActivity thresholds early**, often all at once at interval start. The monitor drops any event that would mean phone time grew faster than the clock since the last milestone (or since midnight).
2. **Goal changed mid-day:** monitoring restarts with new thresholds and `includesPastActivity` (iOS 17.4+), so today's past use still counts.
3. **Permission denied, or no selection:** Today shows the Screen Time card with a "Turn on Screen Time" button, never a crash or 0 m pretending to be real.
4. **Milestones from yesterday** never count today: they're filtered by local day.

## Tasks

1. **Milestone maths (TDD):** `src/screentime/milestones.ts`.
   - `thresholds(goalMin)`: sorted unique whole minutes, at least 1, at most 40.
   - `phoneMinToday(list, nowMs)`: the highest milestone with today's local date.
   - `islandPhone(phoneMin, goalMin)`: the progress line and mood.
   - Tests come first.
2. **Swift module and native view:** `modules/daily-native/ios/DailyScreenTimeModule.swift` and the JS wrapper `modules/daily-native/screenTime.ts`, with a no-op off iOS.
3. **Extensions:**
   - `targets/activity-monitor`: the monitor with the clock guard.
   - `targets/screentime-report`: the Today and Week scenes.
   - The apple-targets patch: the report type, plus the `.appex` product path for ExtensionKit.
4. **App wiring:**
   - `FEATURES.screenTime`
   - onboarding step 2 on iPhone: permission, picker, start
   - Today: the report view, plus the milestone headline, mood and Sunny card
   - Week: the report view
   - Settings: "What counts" and the goal change restarting monitoring
   - Island: progress shows phone time and mood
   - app.json entitlements
5. **Build to TestFlight and check on the device:**
   - the permission flow
   - Today's numbers against iOS Settings → Screen Time
   - Week
   - a milestone after the goal × 10% of use
   - the Island compact figure
